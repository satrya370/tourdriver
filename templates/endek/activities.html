Places worth the drive around Ubud

Seven places Wayan drives guests to most often, with how long to stay, when to go and how to get there.

Ubud and around

01

Tegallalang rice terraces

Tegallalang, Gianyar

Terraced rice fields on a steep valley wall, with paths down into the paddies.

Suggested duration
1 to 1,5 hours
Best time
Early morning, before 09:00
Getting there
About 25 minutes by car from Ubud centre.
Nearby
Swing and coffee plantations along the same road

Local tip · Arrive before 09:00 for empty paths. · Wear shoes with grip.

02

Tirta Empul

Tampaksiring, Gianyar

A holy spring temple where Balinese families come to purify in the pools.

Suggested duration
1,5 hours
Best time
Before 11:00
Getting there
About 40 minutes by car from Ubud.
Nearby
Gunung Kawi, Tampaksiring palace

Local tip · Sarong required, rent one at the gate. · Bring a change of clothes to join the ritual.

03

Goa Gajah

Bedulu, Gianyar

A rock-cut temple cave and bathing pools from the 11th century.

Suggested duration
1 hour
Best time
Afternoon
Getting there
About 15 minutes by car from Ubud.
Nearby
Yeh Pulu relief

Local tip · Cover shoulders and knees.

Highlands and volcano

04

Mount Batur and the Kintamani rim

Kintamani, Bangli

A volcano and crater lake seen from the rim road. Sunrise is the main reason to go.

Suggested duration
3 to 4 hours with breakfast
Best time
Sunrise, April to October
Getting there
About 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Ubud.
Nearby
Penglipuran village, Tegenungan

Local tip · Dress in layers. It is about 15 °C before 06:00. · Book breakfast with a view at a rim warung.

05

Ulun Danu Beratan

Bedugul, Tabanan

A temple complex on the shore of a highland lake.

Suggested duration
1 to 1,5 hours
Best time
Early morning
Getting there
About 1 hour 45 minutes by car from Ubud.
Nearby
Handara gate, Jatiluwih terraces

Local tip · Go before 10:00 to avoid tour buses. · Bring a light jacket.

Coast

06

Uluwatu temple

Uluwatu, Badung

A sea temple on a cliff, famous for sunset and the kecak dance.

Suggested duration
2 to 3 hours with the dance
Best time
Late afternoon to sunset
Getting there
About 1 hour 45 minutes by car from Ubud.
Nearby
Padang Padang, Jimbaran

Local tip · Keep sunglasses and phones away from the monkeys. · Wear long trousers or a sarong.

07

Tanah Lot

Beraban, Tabanan

A sea temple on a rock, reachable on foot at low tide.

Suggested duration
1,5 to 2 hours
Best time
Late afternoon
Getting there
About 1 hour 15 minutes by car from Ubud.
Nearby
Pererenan beach

Local tip · Check the tide before you go. · Arrive before 16:00 to avoid the sunset crowd.

Tour questions

Before you book.

Can I change the order of the stops? +

Yes. Wayan may suggest a different order to avoid crowds, and tells you before changing anything.

Are entrance tickets included? +

No. Tickets are paid at each place. Wayan can tell you the current prices before you go.

Can a tour be shorter or longer? +

Yes. Extra hours are IDR 60.000 each. Shorter trips are charged at the half-day rate.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Cancel up to 24 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later cancellations are charged half the day rate.
