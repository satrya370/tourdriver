08 / Before we go

A few things
worth knowing.

Can we change the route on the day?+

Absolutely. We can adjust stops to your pace, the weather, and traffic. If a big detour changes the price, we'll agree on it first.

Is the price per person or per car?+

Per car, for up to four guests. Petrol, parking, and bottled water are included. Entrance tickets, meals, and activities are separate.

Are children welcome?+

Yes. Tell me your children's ages when you enquire so we can plan breaks and discuss child-seat availability before you book.

What if our plans change?+

Message me as soon as you know. Rescheduling and cancellation terms are confirmed with your quote, before you make a booking.

How early is a Kintamani sunrise pickup?+

Usually around 3:30 am from Ubud. Your exact pickup depends on your hotel, the route, and the season. I'll confirm it the day before.
