Before you book

A few things
worth knowing.

Can we change the route on the day?+

Absolutely. We can adjust stops to your pace, the weather, and traffic. If a big detour changes the price, we agree on it first.

Is the price per person or per car?+

Per car, for up to four guests. Petrol, parking, and bottled water are included. Entrance tickets, meals, and activities are separate.

How many days can a multi-day trip run?+

Between two and fourteen days. Longer than that and I'd rather split it into two trips than rush you.

What if it rains?+

We swap the outdoor stops for indoor ones like the art market, a temple blessing, or a longer lunch. It happens often, and it never ruins the day.

Can you take us off the usual route?+

That's the point. Some of my favourite stops are not on any tourist list, and I won't take commission from shops we pass.
