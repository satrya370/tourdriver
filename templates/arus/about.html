Most guests book me for one day and come back for the next trip. This page covers who you'll be riding with, and how I work.

Wayan Sudiarta

Local driver & island enthusiast

W.
Illustrative portrait

I was born in Ubud and started driving for my uncle's guests in 2014. Back then I knew three routes. Now I know the ones that dodge the market traffic on Saturdays.

Families book me because the pace suits them. Couples ask for viewpoints without rushing. Solo travellers usually want the roads I've driven a thousand times, and the stops I actually like.

I speak English and Bahasa Indonesia, and I'm happy to explain temple etiquette, the Galungan calendar, and why we leave small offerings on the car dashboard before a long drive.

What I don't do is take commission from shops. If I recommend a place, it's because I eat there too.

English & Bahasa IndonesiaBased in Ubud11 years driving

How I work

Three things I do
on every trip.

01

On time, every time

I arrive ten minutes before pickup. If traffic changes the plan, you hear it from me first, not after.

02

Safe, clean car

Serviced every 5,000 km. Tyres, brakes, and air conditioning are checked before the first trip of the day.

03

A flexible route

Tell me what you want to see. I suggest an order that avoids the crowds, and I never take commission from shops.
