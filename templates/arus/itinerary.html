Trip overview

The shape of the three days

Three days that start early and end late, with enough room in the middle for the parts nobody planned. The first morning is for the volcano, because the light there is worth the early start; the second slows down in Ubud; the third heads south and ends at the sea.

Day 1

Above the clouds

Kintamani · Tegallalang

  1. 03.30 · Pickup · transport

    Hotel pickup in Ubud

    The road is empty before four. Sleep in the car if you like.

  2. 05.30 · Viewpoint · sightseeing

    The lake at first light · Kintamani

    Find a spot early on the rim road. Coffee and eggs are sold at the terrace stalls.

    Local tip · bring a warm layer. It is genuinely cold at the rim before sunrise.

  3. 10.00 · Village · activity

    Tegallalang bamboo village

    Walk the main lane and see the old houses. Entrance ticket paid on site.

  4. 13.00 · Lunch · food

    Lunch with a valley view

    One of several terrace restaurants on the road. Lunch is not included.

Day 2

Green paths and quiet temples

Ubud · Tegallalang · Tirta Empul

  1. 08.00 · Pickup · transport

    Hotel pickup

    Breakfast at the hotel first, then we leave.

  2. 09.30 · Terraces · sightseeing

    The rice terraces, before the crowds

    We go down into the paddies, not only to the top platform.

  3. 12.00 · Temple · sightseeing

    Tirta Empul spring temple

    A sarong is required. Bring a change of clothes and a dry bag for the phone.

    Local tip · the queue is shortest just after lunch, before the tour buses arrive.

  4. 16.00 · Free time · free

    The market, or a longer coffee

    Where you spend this time is your call. The car waits, and I have a coffee place in mind.

Day 3

The south coast and the last light

Nusa Dua · Tabanan

  1. 14.00 · Beach · free

    Free time at Nusa Dua

    Two hours. The car waits in the paid lot.

  2. 17.00 · Sunset · sightseeing

    Sea temple and the Kecak dance

    Tickets for the dance are bought on site. Wear long trousers and a sarong.

  3. 19.30 · Drop-off · transport

    Back to your hotel

    Traffic from the south coast can take an hour after the show. We leave in good time.

Transfer note · The south coast road is slow after the performance. Expect 60 minutes back.

Explore places

Places worth your time

Central Ubud

Tegallalang rice terraces

The carved slopes above Ubud, still worked by the subak. Go early or late in the day, when the light is on the water rather than the crowds.

Suggested duration
1.5–2 hours
Best time
Early morning
Getting there
25 minutes from Ubud
Nearby
Tirta Empul

Local tip · the walk down into the paddies is the good part; most people turn back at the top platform.

South coast

Sea temple at Tanah Lot

A temple on the rocks where the sun drops into the sea, and the Kecak dancers performing as the sky turns. Busy, and worth it.

Suggested duration
2 hours
Best time
One hour before sunset
Getting there
45 minutes from Ubud
Nearby
Nusa Dua beaches

Local tip · park at the first car park and walk down; the upper rows fill up fast.
