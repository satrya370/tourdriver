Bali at a glance

The basics before you book.

Bali is a small island with a big range: volcano rims at 1.500 metres, rice terraces, reef beaches and temples on cliffs.

Currency
Rupiah (IDR)
Language
Bahasa Indonesia, Balinese
Time zone
WITA (UTC+8)
Dry season
April to October
Temperature
27–32 °C on the coast
Airport
Ngurah Rai (DPS)

Higher areas such as Kintamani are 8–10 °C cooler than the coast, especially at sunrise.

Things to do

Places worth the drive.

  1. 01

    Tegallalang rice terraces

    Go before 08.00 for empty paths. Walk down into the paddies, not only the top platform.

  2. 02

    Tirta Empul

    A spring temple where Balinese families come to purify. Sarong required, bring a change of clothes.

  3. 03

    Mount Batur at sunrise

    Drive to the Kintamani rim, or hike the volcano with a local guide. Cold at the top: bring a layer.

  4. 04

    Uluwatu temple and kecak

    Cliff temple at sunset. Keep sunglasses and phones away from the monkeys.

  5. 05

    Sidemen valley

    Quiet terraces and weaving workshops, about 1,5 hours from Ubud. Good for a slow day.

  6. 06

    Nusa Penida day trip

    I drive you to the Sanur harbour and wait for your return boat. The island roads need a local driver there.

What to eat

Ask me where I eat.

Warungs (small family restaurants) serve the best Balinese food. Meals cost IDR 25.000–60.000 per person.

  • Babi guling

    Spit-roasted pork with rice and spiced vegetables. Eat it before noon, it often sells out.

  • Nasi campur

    Rice with small portions of meat, vegetables and sambal. The easiest first order.

  • Bebek betutu

    Slow-cooked duck in spices. Many places need it ordered a day ahead.

  • Sate lilit

    Minced fish or chicken on lemongrass sticks, grilled over coconut husk.

What to pack

Small things that help.

  • Shoulder and knee cover

    Temples ask for it. A sarong is lent or sold at the gate, but your own is more comfortable.

  • A warm layer

    Kintamani at 05.00 is about 15 °C. The car's air conditioning is also cold.

  • Small rupiah notes

    Tickets, parking at viewpoints and warungs often do not accept cards or large notes.

  • Rain jacket (November to March)

    Showers are short and heavy in the afternoon. Plan indoor stops after 15.00.

  • Reef-safe sunscreen

    The sun is strong even when it is cloudy. Reapply at the beach and the terraces.

  • Closed shoes for walking

    Terrace paths and temple steps are uneven and slippery after rain.

Questions

Things guests ask first.

When is the best time to visit Bali? +

April to October is the dry season, with clearer skies for sunrise trips. November to March is wetter but greener, with fewer crowds at the temples.

What time do you start for Kintamani sunrise? +

We leave Ubud around 03.30. Pickup from most hotels in Ubud takes about 20 minutes.

Is overtime charged per hour? +

Yes, IDR 60.000 per extra hour. We tell you before you go over, never after.

Can you take a child seat? +

Yes, free of charge. Tell us the child's age when you book so we bring the right seat.

How do I pay? +

Bank transfer, or cash in IDR at the end of the day. We do not take a deposit for full-day bookings.

What if I cancel? +

Cancel 24 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later cancellations are charged half the day rate.
