Questions

Things guests ask first.

When is the best time to visit Bali? +

April to October is the dry season, with clearer skies for sunrise trips. November to March is wetter but greener, with fewer crowds at the temples.

What time do you start for Kintamani sunrise? +

We leave Ubud around 03.30. Pickup from most hotels in Ubud takes about 20 minutes.

Is overtime charged per hour? +

Yes, IDR 60.000 per extra hour. We tell you before you go over, never after.

Can you take a child seat? +

Yes, free of charge. Tell us the child's age when you book so we bring the right seat.

How do I pay? +

Bank transfer, or cash in IDR at the end of the day. We do not take a deposit for full-day bookings.

What if I cancel? +

Cancel 24 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later cancellations are charged half the day rate.

Send your dates.

We reply within the hour, 06.00–21.00 WITA.
