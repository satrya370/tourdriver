Kintamani Sunrise

Drive up before dawn to the volcano rim. Coffee at the viewpoint, then the slow road back through villages. Back in Ubud before lunch.

  1. 1Penelokan viewpoint. Mount Batur and the crater lake at first light.
  2. 2Breakfast at the rim. Coffee and eggs at a terrace warung.
  3. 3Penglipuran village. Bamboo forest and old houses. Ticket paid on site.

Tip: bring a warm layer. It is about 15 °C at the rim before 06.00.

Ubud Terraces and Villages

A full day of the Ubud highlands: terraces, craft villages and a spring temple, with a long lunch in between.

  1. 1Tegallalang rice terraces. Arrive before 09.00 for empty paths.
  2. 2Mas woodcarving village. Watch carvers work. No pressure to buy.
  3. 3Tirta Empul. Sarong required. Bring a change of clothes to join the purification.
  4. 4Lunch with a valley view. Lunch is not included.
  5. 5Goa Gajah. Rock-cut temple cave, on the way back to Ubud.

Uluwatu Sunset

The cliff temple at golden hour, then the kecak fire dance. Drop-off at your hotel in the evening.

  1. 1Uluwatu temple. Keep sunglasses and phones away from the monkeys.
  2. 2Kecak dance. Tickets are bought on site. Wear long trousers.

Tip: traffic from Uluwatu can take 60 minutes after the show. Plan dinner near the temple.

Tour questions

Before you book.

Can I change the order of the stops? +

Yes. Wayan may suggest a different order to avoid crowds, and tells you before changing anything.

Are entrance tickets included? +

No. Tickets are paid at each place. Wayan can tell you the current prices before you go.

Can a tour be shorter or longer? +

Yes. Extra hours are IDR 60.000 each. Shorter trips are charged at the half-day rate.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Cancel up to 24 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later cancellations are charged half the day rate.
