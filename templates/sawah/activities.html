Sunrise · 6 hours · pickup 03.30

Kintamani Sunrise

Drive up before dawn to the volcano rim. Coffee at the viewpoint, then the slow road back through villages. Back in Ubud before lunch.

  1. Penelokan viewpoint. Mount Batur and the crater lake at first light.
  2. Breakfast at the rim. Coffee and eggs at a terrace warung.
  3. Penglipuran village. Bamboo forest and traditional houses. Entrance ticket paid on site.

Tip: bring a warm layer. It is about 15 °C at the rim before 06.00.

Culture · 10 hours · pickup 08.00

Ubud Terraces and Villages

A full day of the Ubud highlands: terraces, craft villages and a spring temple, with a long lunch in between.

  1. Tegallalang rice terraces. Arrive before 09.00 for empty paths.
  2. Mas woodcarving village. Watch carvers work. No pressure to buy.
  3. Tirta Empul. Sarong required. Bring a change of clothes if you want to join the purification.
  4. Lunch with a valley view. Lunch is not included.
  5. Goa Gajah. Rock-cut temple cave, on the way back to Ubud.

Coast · 5 hours · pickup 15.00

Uluwatu Sunset

The cliff temple at golden hour, then the kecak fire dance. Drop-off at your hotel in the evening.

  1. Uluwatu temple. Keep sunglasses and phones away from the monkeys.
  2. Kecak dance. Tickets are bought on site. Wear long trousers.

Tip: traffic from Uluwatu can take 60 minutes after the show. Plan dinner near the temple.

Temples · 10 hours · pickup 08.00

Bedugul and Tanah Lot

The lake temple in the cool highlands in the morning, the sea temple at sunset.

  1. Ulun Danu Beratan. Best before 10.00, before the tour buses arrive.
  2. Lunch near the lake. Lunch is not included.
  3. Tanah Lot at sunset. The temple is reachable at low tide only. Wayan checks the tide for your date.
