Three days in Bali: Ubud, the volcano and the south coast

Bali · 12–14 March 2027 · 2 adults, 1 child

A slow first trip: early starts for the best light, long lunches in between, and the drive back to Ubud before the afternoon traffic.

Day 1

Kintamani sunrise and the terraces

Kintamani, Tegallalang

  1. 03.30 · Pickup

    Hotel pickup in Ubud

    The road is empty before 04.00. Sleep in the car if you like.

    Tip: bring a warm layer. It is cold at the rim.

  2. 05.30 · Viewpoint

    Penelokan, Mount Batur at sunrise

    Find a spot early on the rim road. Coffee and eggs are sold at the terrace stalls.

  3. 10.00 · Village

    Penglipuran bamboo village

    Walk the main lane and see the old houses. Entrance ticket paid on site.

  4. 13.00 · Lunch

    Lunch with a valley view

    Tegallalang, one of several terrace restaurants on the road. Lunch not included.

Stay

Ubud guest house

Penestanan, Ubud

Meals

Breakfast at hotel · Lunch and dinner on your own

Day 2

Ubud art and temples

Ubud, Tegallalang, Tirta Empul

  1. 08.00 · Pickup

    Hotel pickup

    Breakfast at the hotel first, then we leave.

  2. 09.30 · Terraces

    Tegallalang rice terraces

    Go down to the paddies, not only the top platform.

  3. 12.00 · Temple

    Tirta Empul spring temple

    Sarong required. Bring a change of clothes.

Day 3

South coast and sunset

Nusa Dua, Uluwatu

  1. 14.00 · Beach

    Free time at Nusa Dua beach

    Two hours. The car waits in the paid lot.

  2. 17.00 · Sunset

    Uluwatu temple and kecak dance

    Tickets for the dance are bought on site. Wear long trousers.

  3. 19.30 · Drop-off

    Back to your hotel

    Traffic from Uluwatu can take 60 minutes after the show.
