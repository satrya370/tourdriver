Questions

Things guests ask first.

What time do you start for Kintamani sunrise? +

We leave Ubud around 03.30. Pickup from most hotels in Ubud takes about 20 minutes.

Is petrol and parking included? +

Yes. Full-day and half-day charters include petrol, parking and bottled water. Entrance tickets and meals are paid by you.

What if I need more than 10 hours? +

IDR 60.000 per extra hour. We tell you before you go over, never after.

Can you bring a child seat? +

Yes, free of charge. Tell us the child's age when you book.

How do I pay? +

Bank transfer, or cash in IDR at the end of the day. No deposit for full-day bookings.

What is your cancellation policy? +

Cancel up to 24 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later cancellations are charged half the day rate.

Send your dates. We reply within the hour.

Every day, 06.00–21.00 WITA. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup.
