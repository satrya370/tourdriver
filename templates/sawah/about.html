Eleven years on the same roads, mostly on time.

Hi, I am Wayan. I drive guests around Ubud, Kintamani and the south coast of Bali. This page is about how I work and what you can expect.

Message Wayan See tours
[FOTO: Wayan tersenyum di samping mobil, portrait 4:5]

Born in Ubud, I started driving for my uncle's guests in 2014. Back then I knew three roads. Now I know which ones avoid the Saturday market traffic.

Most guests book one day and then a second. Families ask for a slower pace; couples ask for the viewpoints nobody else stops at.

I speak English and Bahasa Indonesia, and I can explain temple etiquette and the ceremony calendar on the way.

How I work

Three things I do on every trip.

  1. 1

    On time, every time

    I arrive 10 minutes before pickup. If traffic changes the plan, you hear it from me first.

  2. 2

    A safe, clean car

    The car is serviced every 5.000 km. I check tyres and brakes before the first trip of the day.

  3. 3

    Honest advice

    I suggest an order that avoids the crowds, and I take no commission from shops.
