Day 1 · Mar 12, 2027

Kintamani sunrise and the terraces

Kintamani, Penglipuran, Tegallalang

  1. 03:30Transport1 hr

    Hotel pickup and drive north

    Wayan collects you at the hotel lobby. The road is empty before 04:00, so you can rest in the car.

    Ubud

    Local tip · Bring a warm layer. It is about 15 °C at the rim.

  2. 05:30Sightseeing2 hr

    Mount Batur at sunrise

    Find a spot on the crater rim before first light. Mount Batur and the lake come into view as the sky turns orange.

    Penelokan viewpoint, Kintamani

    Local tip · Arrive before 05:45 for the best spots.

  3. 07:30Food1 hr

    Breakfast at the rim

    Coffee, eggs and fried banana at a family warung with a view of the caldera.

    Terrace warung, Kintamani

  4. 09:00Sightseeing1 hr 30 min

    Penglipuran bamboo village

    Walk the main lane of the traditional village and see the matching family compounds. The village ticket is paid on site.

    Penglipuran, Bangli

    Local tip · Ask before taking photos inside family compounds.

  5. 11:00Food1 hr 15 min

    Lunch with a valley view

    Lunch at a terrace restaurant above the rice paddies. Lunch is not included in the price.

    Tegallalang

  6. 12:30Sightseeing1 hr 30 min

    Tegallalang rice terraces

    Walk down into the paddies, not only the top platform. Wayan knows the quieter paths.

    Tegallalang

    Local tip · Wear shoes with grip. The steps are uneven.

  7. 14:30Transport45 min

    Return to Ubud

    Back at your hotel by about 15:15, in time to rest before dinner.

    Ubud

Meals: Breakfast

Hotel: Ubud guest house

Penestanan, Ubud · Same hotel for Day 1 and Day 2.

Day 2 · Mar 13, 2027

Ubud art and temples

Ubud, Mas and Tirta Empul

  1. 08:00Transport30 min

    Hotel pickup

    Breakfast at the hotel first, then Wayan collects you.

    Ubud

  2. 09:00Sightseeing1 hr 30 min

    Mas woodcarving village

    Watch carvers at work in family studios. There is no pressure to buy.

    Mas, Gianyar

  3. 11:00Activity1 hr 30 min

    Tirta Empul spring temple

    Visit the holy spring where Balinese families come to purify. You can join the ritual or simply watch.

    Tampaksiring

    Local tip · Sarong required. Bring a change of clothes and go before 11:00 for a quieter pool.

  4. 13:00Food1 hr 15 min

    Lunch in Tampaksiring

    A simple warung lunch. Ask Wayan for his favourite nasi campur.

    Tampaksiring

  5. 14:30Sightseeing1 hr 15 min

    Goa Gajah

    A rock-cut temple cave and bathing pools from the 11th century, on the way back.

    Bedulu, Gianyar

  6. 16:00Free time1 hr

    Free time in Ubud town

    Wayan drops you in the centre for shops and cafes and picks you up again at 17:00.

    Ubud centre

  7. 17:00Transport30 min

    Return to the hotel

    Back at your hotel by about 17:30.

    Ubud

Meals: Breakfast

Hotel: Ubud guest house

Penestanan, Ubud

Day 3 · Mar 14, 2027

South coast and sunset

Nusa Dua and Uluwatu

  1. 13:30Transport1 hr 30 min

    Drive to the south coast

    A relaxed drive south, with a stop for coffee if you like.

    Ubud to Nusa Dua

  2. 15:00Free time2 hr

    Beach time at Nusa Dua

    Two hours on the beach. The car waits in the paid parking lot.

    Nusa Dua

    Local tip · Reef-safe sunscreen and a hat help in the afternoon sun.

  3. 17:00Sightseeing1 hr 30 min

    Uluwatu temple at golden hour

    Walk the cliff path to the sea temple as the light turns gold.

    Uluwatu

    Local tip · Keep sunglasses and phones away from the monkeys.

  4. 18:30Activity1 hr

    Kecak fire dance

    A traditional dance performed by dozens of men at sunset. Tickets are bought on site.

    Uluwatu temple amphitheatre

    Local tip · Wear long trousers or a sarong.

  5. 19:30Transport1 hr 30 min

    Drive back to Ubud

    Traffic from Uluwatu can take 60 minutes after the show. Back at your hotel around 21:00.

    Uluwatu to Ubud

Transfer note: Check-out day: if you leave Bali the next morning, Wayan can drive you to the airport (IDR 350.000 from Ubud).

Practical information

Included

  • •Private car and driver for three days
  • •Petrol and parking
  • •Bottled water in the car
  • •English and Bahasa Indonesia speaking driver
  • •Hotel pickup and drop-off in Ubud

Not included

  • •Entrance tickets and the kecak dance ticket
  • •Meals and drinks
  • •Hotel accommodation
  • •Tip for the driver (optional)

What to bring

  • •A warm layer for the Kintamani sunrise
  • •Comfortable walking shoes
  • •Sarong and sash (or rent at the temple)
  • •Small IDR notes for tickets and parking
  • •Reef-safe sunscreen and a hat

Not suitable for

  • •Guests who cannot be ready at 03:00 on Day 1
  • •Travellers with limited mobility on the Tegallalang steps

Not allowed

  • •Smoking in the car
  • •Durian in the car
  • •Entering temples without a sarong and sash

Local tip

  • •Leave early. Roads in Bali are quieter before 08:00.
  • •Small IDR notes are easier than cards at warungs and ticket booths.
  • •Carry a rain jacket from November to March.
  • •Temples can close for ceremonies. Wayan checks the dates in advance.
