Hi, I am Wayan. Eleven years on the same roads.

I drive guests around Ubud, Kintamani and the south coast of Bali. Here is how I work and what you can expect.

Message WayanSee tours
[FOTO: Wayan tersenyum di samping mobil, portrait 4:5]

I was born in Ubud and started driving for my uncle's guests in 2014. Back then I knew three routes. Now I know the ones that avoid the market traffic on Saturdays.

Most guests book me for one day and come back for the next trip. Families like the slower pace. Couples ask for a route that stops at the right viewpoints without rushing.

I speak English and Bahasa Indonesia. I can explain temple etiquette, the ceremony calendar, and why we leave small offerings on the car dashboard.

How I work

Three things I do on every trip.

  1. 1

    On time, every time

    I arrive 10 minutes before pickup. If traffic changes the plan, you hear it from me first.

  2. 2

    A safe, clean car

    The car is serviced every 5.000 km. I check tyres and brakes before the first trip of the day.

  3. 3

    Honest advice

    I suggest an order that avoids the crowds, and I take no commission from shops.
